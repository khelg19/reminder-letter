A Little Reminder For You

Whenever life feels a little heavier than usual, come back and read this.

hey Janelle,

i just want to remind you of something.

alam ko minsan sobrang hirap ng buhay. may mga araw na pagod na pagod ka na, at may mga pagkakataon na parang gusto mo na lang sumuko kasi feeling mo ang dami mong dinadala. pero sana huwag mong kalimutan na lahat ng ginagawa mo ngayon has a purpose.

every late night, every exam, every struggle, at bawat sacrifice mo, hindi yan masasayang. one day, marerealize mo na lahat ng pinagdaanan mo was preparing you for the future you've always prayed for. kahit mabagal ang progress, ang importante is tuloy tuloy ka lang.

please don't be too hard on yourself. hindi mo kailangang maging perfect. just keep showing up, keep learning, and keep believing that better days are coming. trust the process. your future self will thank you for not giving up today.

kapag feeling mo napag iiwanan ka na, remember that life is not a race. everyone has their own journey at sariling timing. don't compare yourself to others. focus on becoming a better version of yourself little by little, every single day.

and please, always believe in yourself. kahit sa mga araw na nahihirapan kang maniwala sa sarili mo, sana maalala mo na may taong naniniwala sa'yo. isa na ako doon.

kung dumating man yung point na sobrang bigat na ng lahat, don't keep everything to yourself. hindi mo kailangang dalhin lahat mag isa. if you ever need someone to listen, someone who understands you, or someone na sasamahan ka kahit tahimik lang, i'm here. meron kang ako. i may not have all the answers, but i'll always make time to listen.

okay lang mapagod. okay lang umiyak. okay lang magpahinga. but please, don't give up on your dreams. isipin mo yung araw na hawak mo na yung diploma mo, successful ka na sa career mo, at masasabi mo sa sarili mo, "kinaya ko." darating din yung araw na yun, at magiging worth it lahat ng pinagdadaanan mo ngayon.

take care of yourself too. don't forget to eat, get enough rest, and always choose yourself kapag masyado nang overwhelming ang lahat. you matter more than you think.

always remember this. storms don't last forever. no matter how difficult life gets, the sun will always rise again. and when that day comes, you'll be glad that you chose to keep going. (sabi sa search :) )

so keep your head up. keep fighting. keep believing. your dreams are waiting for you, at alam kong maaabot mo rin sila.

and whenever life feels too heavy, just remember...

meron kang ako. KAYA MO YANNNNNN

🌸 Always cheering for you. Keep believing. Keep going. One day you'll look back and be proud that you never gave up. 🌸
